Coffee Facts

Goat discovery: Legend says a 9th-century Ethiopian goat herder named Kaldi found coffee after his goats ate energetic red berries.
Not a true bean: Coffee "beans" are actually the seeds inside a bright red fruit called a coffee cherry.
Global bans: Rulers in Mecca and Europe tried to ban coffee in past centuries because they feared it caused radical thinking.

Coffee Varieties

Pour Over 

Hot water drips slowly through coffee grounds held in a paper filter.

French Press

Coarse coffee grounds steep directly in hot water before a mesh plunger presses them to the bottom.

Espresso

High-pressure hot water forces through tightly packed, finely ground coffee to make a concentrated shot.
